# Character creator quality handoff

The round 18 Game exports scored **65.6/100 for finish and 77.8/100 for visible diversity** against a target of 80 on each axis. Adult finish is the largest gap, at 55.7 against Dragon's Dogma 2 and 66.0 against The Sims 4. Creature finish is 75.3 against Spore. One independent reviewer scored 68 exported characters across 60 sheets. Their estimated uncertainty is about six points on overall finish.

## What changed through round 18

| Area | Result | Engineering decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinary adult hair | On held-out portraits, reducing contrast in curly hair erased much of the shape that lets a viewer recognize coils. Adding twisted groups produced strips sticking above the silhouette. Independent reviewers preferred the existing groom in every clear decision on the first test and in five of eight comparisons on the second, with three ties. | Keep the existing curly styles. A future treatment needs readable groups inside the hair and a soft outline through a turn. |
| Warden hair, beard and eyes | Rooted swept locks give the hairline depth. Gaze-following lids keep pupils readable as the eyes move. The player reapplies user gaze after sampling animation. Skin-rooted beard clumps follow the jaw and expressions. Short moustache cards still break into flakes beneath the nostrils. | Keep the rooted warden hair and gaze changes. A Vibe Human-inspired comb shared by skin paint and cards produced mixed blind rankings. The lip clearance rule removed 34 of 40 eligible cards nearest the warden's lip in Neutral. Allowing the tips 1.5 mm past the clearance line filled the stripe in the warden's tested neutral and Smile views. The angular cards remained, a regional density test failed and the assessor crop was inconclusive. Keep the trial private. |
| Skin | The weathered warden's red cheek streaks came from layered colour. On ordinary cast faces, a broad area with low roughness produced the waxy highlight under direct light. Lifting only the glossiest skin texels reduced the highlight on two heads. The smooth cheek colour remained visible in unlit renders. Fine roughness grain added to the lift made no visible difference on either head under two lights. | Treat weathering, fine skin variation and highlight shape separately. Compare both cheeks at matched pupil spacing against the photographed face and DD2 Geralt under recorded lighting. |
| Creature coats | Thinning the original fur left about a third of its strands as isolated wires. At the same triangle allowance, wider guard clumps over short undercoat tufts read as fur in blind review on two subjects. The difference was small at 180 px. | Retain the Game pelt. Feather-normal tilts added little structure, and painting overlap shadow at only three vertices across a vane darkened too much of it. |
| Export cost | In six adult comparisons, doubling both the Game triangle allowance and atlas width bought little at whole-figure size. The larger atlas quadrupled decoded texture memory. Restoring original coil tubes at 24k displaced enough face geometry to break three faces. | Keep the compact default. Test any larger preset on a named feature with face, hair and clothing budgets measured separately. |
| Cast variety | In 61 of 333 consecutive six-person groups, two adults wore the same cast identity and fitted clothes. Five of the eight cast members can wear only their original outfit. Skin and body edits can still distinguish the pair. | Count visible outfits on the rendered cast when measuring diversity. Native hair and wardrobe choices stored underneath a cast figure can exaggerate the range. |

## What the scores tell us

Each game carries one third of the overall score. Within a game the weights are 30% fresh consecutive draws, 30% recurring draws and 40% curated figures. The 13 reference originals anchor each game's best work at 95. The full benchmark has 68 exported characters and 60 sheets in the standard game player. The target budget is 24,000 triangles with the exporter's four-triangle tolerance, one material and 1024-square maps.

Several small improvements are real at the level of an individual character. Absolute scores fluctuate across jurors and fresh draws. Between rounds 12 and 13 all eight curated-adult sheets were byte-identical. Different ratings of those pictures alone account for 3.07 points of the decrease in overall finish. In a separate source study, the same Gardener GLB scored 60 and 80 against DD2 in different panels. For attributing a visual improvement, the strongest evidence is a blind comparison of the same person in the same views within one review. ## Approaches in the editable workshop

| Approach | What worked | Where it fell short and why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Native human shape and facial anatomy | Body measurements, age and weight produce recognizably different people. Mesh protection preserved the shelf and thickness of upper eyelids through game reduction. Six male face foundations strengthened cheek, jaw and nose planes while keeping native eyes and expressions. | Very close body fits still missed facial likeness. On random draws the foundation change is modest beside age and weight. The attempted female transfers hardened the face or barely moved it, so those four were discarded. [Body fit](human-benchmark.md), eyelid evidence (`.reference-cache/neon-lids8/HANDOFF.md`), shape trial (`.reference-cache/cast-shapes10/HANDOFF.md`). |
| Native and selectable hair | Refitting bobs from the authored mesh retained a rounded volume. The previous triangle-by-triangle fit rippled the shell; scattered tip cards created visible teeth. Untwisting long waves reduced gaps. Clumped Afro, Curls and Rounded coils gained coherence. | Natural curls could hide ears and read like a wig. Coiled crop sometimes left a ragged nape. The developer judged the bob comparisons, and a final contrast change followed inspection. Scanned short cuts and the Ribbon ponytail stayed optional after mixed comparisons or changes of identity on balding wearers. [Grooms](human-grooms.md), bob trial (`.reference-cache/native-bobs14/README.md`), waves (`.reference-cache/waves9/HANDOFF.md`), [scanned cuts](human-scan-hair.md). |
| Complete Rocketbox adults | Keeping original head, hair and clothes together gained **21.5, 18.5 and 12.5** points over three native comparisons, averaged across the adult anchors. The renderer preserves expressions and a common deformation field keeps lids, lips and collars together through edits. Eight identities, fitted outfit swaps on three, skin/eye colour and limited face blending work in the editor. | One native comparison was only stature-matched. Fine face edits are subtle, clothing exchange is limited, and repeated source identities are obvious. In each of rounds 12-14, four of 24 ordinary adults and zero of four curated adults used this route. That reaches only 10% of the weighted adult lineup. Its 17.5-point average gain on the three subjects would amount to about 1.2 overall under a simple per-person average. Actual juries judge groups. Blind pairs (`.reference-cache/whole-comparison12/RESULTS.md`), coverage count (`.reference-cache/quality-80/approach-comparison15/FINDINGS.md`), [cast controls](human-cast.md). |
| Studio Rain head and source-head grafts | The opt-in Rain family demonstrated a cleanly editable authored face with eye colour, expressions and saved native choices. It can wear chosen native hair and accessories. | Age, beard and donor hair carry much of a person's identity. A Rain head gave very different people the same youthful read. Blind scan-head trials lost hair silhouettes or exposed a chin/neck seam. Head-only replacement also left photo-real skin above flat native clothing. [Studio family](human-face-family.md), blind head comparison (`.reference-cache/head-comparison12/RESULTS.md`). |
| Garments, armour and cloth motion | Hip-hung drape lets legs move inside a skirt. An authored Einar work coat and two scanned knit tops retained folds and wear when fitted to different bodies. Moving the hoodie skin-hide boundary inside its edges removed cuff wedges and neck gaps. Mail-ring UVs, prefiltered shirt mail, seated shoulder plates and fitted calf straps improved readable details in game exports. | Two held-out robes rose into a slab during Crouch, one exposing a knee. Some robe hems still split. Strong edits and high collars reveal fit faults. Native drape helped one complete-cast long tunic, with a stiff hem and no second long garment validating the method. [Attire](human-attire.md), robe trial (`.reference-cache/cloth-drape8/ROUND9.md`), [armour](human-armour.md), greave checks (`.reference-cache/greaves15/RESULT.md`). |
| Skin, eyes and surface maps | Source-specific masks let cast faces and borrowed hands follow the selected skin. Repainting the short photographed upper-lid strip brought its hue near the cheek on all five held-out members. Normals and roughness restore some relief. | All five lids missed the registered brightness target. Two apparent skin failures had other causes: the pale hands were leather gloves, and the face/chest difference mostly followed lighting. Added glove seams were too small to change the read at 300 px. Skin diagnosis (`.reference-cache/skin-continuity15/HANDOFF.md`), glove trial (`.reference-cache/gloves15/RESULT.md`). |
| Procedural creatures | Distinct body plans, silhouettes and fitted clothing provided the strongest diversity. Shaping muscle, joint and tendon regions and varying colour along the limb improved grazers, birds and arthropods. | Clothes conceal many limb improvements. Thin walkers remain thin; repeated tabards, horns, smooth shading and subtle motion stills cost finish. All 28 recurring creatures have equivalent exports between rounds 14 and 15. [Body plans](creature-body-plans.md), limb trial (`.reference-cache/creature-anatomy11/HANDOFF.md`), recurring-export audit (`.reference-cache/quality-80/matched-exports14-15.json`). |

## Private research and rejected experiments

| Experiment | Observed outcome and decision |
| --- | --- |
| Atlas size, hair transparency and lighting | A 512-square intact scan still read well next to a 1024-square native model. Enlarging native face chart widths on six subjects gave no improvement against the 2048 reference at 300 px. Turning off alpha cutout on one problematic native haircut left its geometric notches. On a different cast haircut, removing coplanar duplicate cards did reduce the checker pattern. Studio lighting improved separation while the mesh and outfit faults remained. The official player stayed fixed. Neon experiments (`.reference-cache/quality-80/neon-first-principles12/DECISION-MEMO.md`), Fable source control (`.reference-cache/quality-80/approach-research12/REPORT.html`), atlas trial (`.reference-cache/atlas15/RESULT.md`), cast hair diagnosis (`.reference-cache/cast-hair17/README.md`). |
| Intact photographs made editable | Keeping a complete photographed person retained convincing age and cloth folds in the source pose. Moving the arms revealed missing sleeve and torso surfaces. Another scan pose recovered the trousers at one hip to 1.0 mm median registration error. Loose striped cloth moved independently of the skeleton. The fitted sweater matched visible cloth to 2.92 mm at the 90th percentile but reached 11.7 times edge stretch in motion. Repacking the atlas restored the face; shoulder holes remained. Both garment cases failed their registered tests. Blind scan views (`.reference-cache/scan-panel15/blind/VERDICT.md`), second pose (`.reference-cache/scan-coverage14/README.md`), fitted garment (`.reference-cache/same-garment15/RESULT.md`). |
| Other complete source families | All five Sprite Fright characters converted coherently at budget. They scored 64-74 on Sims and 40-47 on DD2. Their caricature proportions and young cast limit the adult comparison. Two Ryzom adults kept editable faces and recognisable armour silhouettes, but close views exposed older geometry and painted detail. Smoothing and repainting had limited effect. Among ten figures, including controls, none reached 70 against both games. The closest was the Gardener at 80/69, within one judge's uncertainty of the cutoff. Decoded ten-person panel (`.reference-cache/quality-80/approach-comparison15/RESULT.md`), paint and geometry trials (`.reference-cache/paint15/RESULT.md`). |
| Film realism, alternate editable bases and grooms | The Einar game conversion preserved the silhouette and budget, yet its fused beard and unreadable mechanical arm scored 18 DD2 / 16 Sims. VALID converted faithfully but showed modest source-art detail. Vitruvian carried independent body edits, expressions and a rig; the projected hair cards improved silhouette overlap but still showed squared tips. These were private source tests. MetaHuman was investigated for sourcing and licensing only. Einar blind assessment (`.reference-cache/ceiling-panel14/RESULT.md`), other realism sources (`.reference-cache/realistic-ceiling12/FINDINGS.md`), Vitruvian trial (`.reference-cache/vitruvian15/RESULT.md`), MetaHuman research (`.reference-cache/quality-80/metahuman-research14.md`). |

## Code and stopping point

- Native identity and shape start in [human-form.js](../character-view/human-form.js) and [human-surface.js](../character-view/human-surface.js). [human-wardrobe.js](../character-view/human-wardrobe.js) dresses a draw. [human-mesh.js](../character-view/human-mesh.js) assembles it.
- The complete-character alternative starts in [human-cast-looks.js](../character-view/human-cast-looks.js) for selection, [human-cast.js](../character-view/human-cast.js) for rendering and [human-cast-wardrobe.js](../character-view/human-cast-wardrobe.js) for fitted outfits. [game-export.js](../character-view/game-export.js) applies the budget. [export-form.js](../character-view/export-form.js) writes the GLB.
- Round 15 includes greave fit, upper-lid colour and the photographed-figure surface-map fallback. **614 tests passed. All 68 GLBs and 60 review sheets passed the export audit.** Of 44 recurring characters, 40 have equivalent normalized game exports. Four fixed adults changed. The four curated adults and all recurring creatures are equivalent to round 14. The subsequent independent review scored finish at 72.68 and diversity at 89.07. Its report is in `.reference-cache/quality-80/jury-round15/REPORT.md`. Freeze and test log (`.reference-cache/quality-80/round15-changes.md`), export audit (`.reference-cache/quality-80/round15-integrity.json`).
- The broader cast comparison is parked, with all twelve ordinary subjects and 27 distinct recipes saved across the three treatments. Its two blocks use ordinary draws, native-only versions and whole-cast assignments without repeating an identity within a block. Only partial captures exist. There is no population result or basis here for changing the default draw rate. Registration (`.reference-cache/cast-population16/DECLARED.md`) and saved selections (`.reference-cache/cast-population16/recipes.json`).
