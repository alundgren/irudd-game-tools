## What was taken

The head surface with its UV layout, the eyes, eyebrows, eyelashes, hair, ponytail, hair strand and hairband, and the
skin, hair and eye colour maps. Blink, smile and brow lift were posed with the rig's own controls and stored as shape
changes.

## What was changed

- The head is cut at the neck and joined to the editable adult's neck.
- Every head vertex is tied to a point on the editable adult's skin, so the adult's controls move it.
- The eye surface is closed at the pupil, and the loose pupil cards are removed.
- Colour maps are resized (skin 768, hair 512, eyes 256) and re-tinted to the adult's skin, hair and eye colour.
- The ponytail is bent to hang behind the adult's neck.

The packed data is `character-view/human-studio-head-data.json`. It carries the credit, source, licence and this list
of changes. The original download is not in the repository.

The hair alone is also offered as the hairstyle "Ribbon ponytail" on the native head, fitted to that head and
re-dyed.

Exported files that use the family or the hairstyle name it in `asset.copyright` and in `asset.extras.faceFamily` or `asset.extras.studioHair`.
