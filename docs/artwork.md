# Artwork and licenses

The procedural study meshes, surface patterns and generated four-view sheets were made for this project. The adult generator also uses assets from the [MakeHuman community (CC0)](licenses/makehuman-cc0.md), [Microsoft Rocketbox (MIT)](https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox) and [Digital Reality Lab’s HSRD-100 (CC BY 4.0)](licenses/hsrd-100-cc-by-4.0.md). The desktop build includes those notices in its licenses directory.

The portrait reference is an 1870 Frederick Douglass photograph by George Francis Schreiber. Its catalog record credits the Library of Congress and reports no known restrictions on publication. [Historical photograph and rights information](https://www.loc.gov/item/2004671911/). Public-domain figure references include [Amelia Earhart beside her Electra](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Amelia_Earhart_-_GPN-2002-000211.jpg) (NASA) and [Jim Thorpe at the 1912 Olympics](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jim_Thorpe,_1912_Summer_Olympics.jpg) (Agence Rol). The fitted athlete uses newly generated front and side artwork guided by the Olympic photograph and a [second 1912 portrait of Thorpe](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jim_Thorpe_1912.jpg). The raster artwork was created with OpenAI’s image generation tool. It is distributed here under the [MIT license](../LICENSE). Three.js provides rendering and glTF import/export under MIT. The standalone builds embed license notices for Three.js and meshoptimizer.

The original rigged avatars are Gardener_Male_01 in Professions and Female_Adult_01, Female_Adult_04, Female_Adult_08, Female_Adult_13, Male_Adult_01, Male_Adult_08 and Male_Adult_15 in Adults. The source geometry, images and authored facial poses are adapted from the Microsoft Rocketbox Avatar Library, Copyright (c) 2020 Microsoft, under the MIT License. Each exported character records its source avatar and any blended donor in `asset.extras.rocketbox.avatars`, alongside the license text. [Library and license](https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox/tree/0943055db6ec570bcef9f2c8b41c9e5467c808f9)

The fitted wardrobe adapts the Blender Foundation's Rain and Einar meshes and textures. The flying cap and goggles use the CC0 FlightHelmet artwork from Microsoft.
