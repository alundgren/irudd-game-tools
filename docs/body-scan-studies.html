Six body scans in the character workshop

Three female and three male adult scans from 3D Scan Store supply the shape targets. The figures below use one jointed body generator. Open a study to orbit the clay surface, nudge a part, change outfits or export a GLB. The public previews do not give calibrated stature. Each study starts at a nominal 1.72 m.

At a nominal 45 degrees, mean silhouette overlap rose from 70.7% for the starting archetype to 81.1% for the fitted bodies. In the six-way comparison, 4 of six fitted oblique outlines matched their own reference best. The score measures the outline, so look at the shoulder slope, hands and chest in the clay views below.

What the arm check caught

On F08 the torso correction bowed the arms between their joints. Preserving the elbow bend removed that artifact. The arm regions still overlapped their source by only 23.0%. Adjusting arm spread and length raised that local overlap to 64.1% and whole-front overlap from 84.0% to 89.2%. Oblique overlap fell from 88.2% to 85.7%. These measurements make the tradeoff visible.

How to read the scores

The fitter compares body height-normalized front and profile silhouettes with horizontal cuts through the actual Three.js triangles. It adjusts torso depth, limb proportions and stance, then fits a smooth 14-level lattice shared by skin, clothing and joint pivots. Three surface settings for chest fullness, lower abdominal volume and muscle relief were authored from the front plates. Each fit was selected using front and profile overlap plus a front-view score for arm regions visibly separated from the torso. A spatial correction carries each arm along its fitted shoulder-to-wrist displacement while preserving the requested elbow bend. Where the arm score fell below 65%, the fitter also tried small changes to arm spread and length, using the same front and profile inputs. Each mask receives one translation and a uniform height scale.

Silhouette overlap is the area of intersection divided by the area of union. The rear silhouette of an orthographic model mirrors its front. The stronger check is a separate oblique scan image. Its camera metadata is unavailable, so the report scores a fixed nominal 45 degrees and also records 30 and 60 degrees. A tilted head, asymmetric stance and hair can all move the contour. The 95th-percentile boundary distance catches some distal errors that whole-body overlap hides.

The silhouettes do not establish the quality of internal anatomical landmarks, joint deformation under load, or facial likeness. All six figures share a simplified head. Score the depth of the rib cage, the distribution of tissue on the abdomen, elbow and wrist transitions, and knee-to-calf taper by inspecting the clay under moving light. The broadest body's outline is substantially easier to recover than its soft-tissue folds.

To reproduce, build the workshop with npm ci and npm run build. Install Python packages numpy scipy opencv-python requests. Run python scripts/body-fidelity.py CACHE --fit to download the previews and fit the figures. Without --fit the script scores the parameters saved in examples/reference-fits.json, which exist only in a local copy. The local cache holds source previews, color-coded overlay PNGs, a local comparison page, new parameters on a fit run, and a JSON report. The SHA-256 hashes in the manifest pin the input plates. Downloaded scan artwork stays outside the repository.
